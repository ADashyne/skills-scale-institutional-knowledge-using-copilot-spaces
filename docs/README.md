# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative, and data-informed approach to project management, with clear ownership and documented artifacts. Projects move through a consistent lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution and tracking, release and deployment, and retrospective and continuous improvement. Risk management and stakeholder communication apply throughout the lifecycle to keep work aligned, visible, and responsive to change.

These documents are the detailed source of truth for each process area. Use this README as the starting point for understanding the overall approach and finding the guide relevant to your work.

## Project Management Lifecycle

1. **Initiation** — Validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
2. **Planning** — Turn the approved initiative into a prioritized backlog, delivery plan, milestones, estimates, dependencies, and a Definition of Done.
3. **Execution and tracking** — Deliver incremental work through project boards, pull requests, testing, regular team rhythms, progress reporting, and blocker escalation.
4. **Risk management and communication** — Identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor risks while keeping stakeholders informed through regular updates and defined escalation paths.
5. **Release and deployment** — Validate acceptance criteria and CI results, deploy through staging and production checks, communicate releases, and use rollback or incident procedures when needed.
6. **Retrospective and continuous improvement** — Capture learnings after sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents, then track a small number of owned improvement actions.
